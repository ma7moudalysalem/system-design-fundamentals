# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This repository is a space for learning and constructive feedback. Everyone is welcome regardless of experience level, background, or how many times they've been asked to "design Twitter" and frozen.

Expected behaviour:
- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions
- Focus on the content, not the person
- Welcome beginners — every expert once thought a load balancer was a black box

Unacceptable behaviour:
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Spam or off-topic promotion
- Dismissing questions as "too basic"

## Enforcement

Issues or comments that violate this standard will be removed. Repeat violations will result in being blocked from the repository.
